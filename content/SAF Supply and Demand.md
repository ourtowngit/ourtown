# SAF Supply and Demand

IATA's current data provides a more precise basis for discussing the gap between aviation's ambitions and available SAF.

## 2026

IATA estimates:

- approximately **2.4 million tonnes** of SAF production in 2026;
- SAF at about **0.8% of aviation fuel use**;
- approximately **500 million tonnes per year** needed by 2050 on IATA's pathway.

Source: [IATA, June 6, 2026](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/06-06-saf-production-volumes-still-disappointing/).

## Why the gap matters to OurTown

The supply gap does not prove that a particular agricultural feedstock will be economical.

It does establish a problem worth solving:

> **Aviation has a large long-term requirement for lower-carbon fuel, while present SAF production remains a small fraction of total jet-fuel use.**

That creates an opportunity to investigate feedstocks, processing, logistics, financing, and ownership structures.
