# Farm Conservation Sub-Division

The proposed action plan calls for identifying and securing a pilot project or demonstration site in McLeod County for a “Farm Conservation Sub-Division.”

## Purpose

The demonstration would model:

- Sustainability metrics.
- Community impacts.
- Soil health management.
- Water quality protection.
- Carbon sequestration practices.
- Agricultural productivity.

## Connection to the Pilot

The site would connect [[Small Farm Development]] with the broader feedstock and bio-energy system.

The whitepaper proposes using farm conservation guidelines to support access to carbon credit programs and incentives.
