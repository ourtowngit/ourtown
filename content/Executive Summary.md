# Executive Summary

The whitepaper proposes a rail-oriented network of oilseed collection hubs and processing facilities to connect agricultural production with the growing Sustainable Aviation Fuel market.

The approach combines:

- Local and regional oilseed aggregation.
- Processing of seed oils and bio-crude.
- Rail-based movement of feedstocks and products.
- A farmer-oriented [[Farm Cooperative Model]].
- Byproduct utilization for animal feed and energy.
- [[Small Farm Development]] alongside established farm operations.
- Workforce education and technology development.
- [[Rural Revitalization]] through locally retained value.

The initial step is a prototype processing facility. The longer-term concept is a scalable network extending along the [[Highway 212 Corridor]] and westward toward the Dakotas.
