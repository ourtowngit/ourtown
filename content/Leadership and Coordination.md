# Leadership and Coordination

The whitepaper states that rural vitality depends on collective effort among citizens, educators, regulators, farmers, farm organizations, private companies, and public entities.

## Coordination Areas

Successful implementation requires coordination across:

- Local, county, and state planning.
- Zoning and land-use policy.
- Transportation infrastructure.
- Incentive programs.
- Agricultural development.
- Business development.
- Community leadership.
- Stakeholder engagement.

## Steering Group

One of the proposed next steps is formation of a steering group to guide and coordinate efforts.

## Shared Direction

The whitepaper also calls for consensus around a Bio-Fuel Cooperative and Rail Oriented Community Development initiative and for consensus and approval of pilot projects in the 212 corridor.
