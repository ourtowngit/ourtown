# Microsoft and Carbon Negative Goals

Microsoft announced in January 2020 that it intended to become **carbon negative by 2030**. It also said that by 2050 it would remove from the atmosphere an amount of carbon equivalent to its direct and electricity-related emissions since the company's founding in 1975. Microsoft simultaneously launched a **$1 billion Climate Innovation Fund**. 

Source: [Microsoft, “Microsoft will be carbon negative by 2030,” January 16, 2020](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2020/01/16/microsoft-will-be-carbon-negative-by-2030/).

## The 2025 midpoint

In February 2025, Microsoft described 2025 as the midpoint of its sustainability journey. It reported progress in renewable-energy procurement, water replenishment, waste, and land protection, while also acknowledging that the path had become harder.

Microsoft's own wording is notable: goals described as a “moonshot” in 2020 were described in 2025 with the observation that **“the moon has gotten further away.”**

Source: [Microsoft, “Progress on the road to 2030,” February 13, 2025](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/02/13/progress-on-the-road-to-2030/).
