# Financing and Incentives

The whitepaper proposes diversified financing for the pilot and subsequent development.

## Funding Sources

Potential sources identified include:

- Public grants.
- Matching funds.
- Private investment.
- Commercial banking.
- Agricultural and community stakeholders.
- State and federal support programs.

## Incentives

The action plan references Minnesota's SAF tax credit incentives and federal support programs, including the Inflation Reduction Act.

## Financial Analysis

The project calls for financial viability and risk assessment studies to identify economic hurdles and develop risk mitigation strategies.

## Local Investment

Acquisition, financing, and equipment leasing programs are proposed with an emphasis on local investment while using available state and federal support.
