# Wasted Industrial Heat

CarbonShare identifies capturing wasted industrial heat energy as another possible pathway for reducing waste and creating local value.

## The Question

Instead of allowing usable thermal energy to leave an industrial process unused, could it be captured and applied to another economic activity?

Potential applications to investigate include crop drying, greenhouses, district heating, agricultural processing, and other local energy systems.

The CarbonShare notes do not identify a specific facility or technology, so this remains an area for future investigation.
