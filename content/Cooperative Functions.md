# Cooperative Functions

The proposed bio-energy-based cooperative is intended to coordinate functions that individual farms may not be able to perform efficiently alone.

## Supply Aggregation

The cooperative would aggregate regional feedstocks and help stabilize availability.

## Processing

It would advance the development and optimization of oilseed processing facilities and other SAF-relevant processing.

## Market Representation

The cooperative would represent farm interests in bio-fuel and bio-energy commodity markets, including market intelligence and advocacy.

## Shared Knowledge and Services

It would facilitate knowledge exchange and shared services related to energy independence, climate resilience, and agricultural development.

## Public Coordination

The cooperative could serve as a liaison with policymakers and regulatory agencies concerning SAF policies, credits, incentives, and funding.
