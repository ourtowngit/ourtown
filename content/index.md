# Closing the Energy Loop in the Rural Farm Economy

The growing demand for [[Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)]] within the airline industry presents a significant opportunity to stimulate economic revitalization in rural communities. 

This digital garden outlines a framework to establish a network of collection hubs and processing facilities for seed-oils. The resulting infrastructure aims to advance energy independence for rural communities and farm operations through localized production of bio-fuels.
