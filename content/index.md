# Closing the Energy Loop in the Rural Farm Economy

The growing demand for [[Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)]] presents an opportunity to connect agricultural production, localized processing, rail infrastructure, renewable energy, and rural economic development.

This digital garden develops the framework in the whitepaper into an interconnected set of topics: a prototype processing facility, a network of [[Collection Hubs]], [[Rail-Oriented Development]], a [[Farm Cooperative Model]], sustainable farm development, workforce education, and rural revitalization.
