# Minnesota Agricultural Processing History

Minnesota's agricultural economy historically included substantial local and regional processing infrastructure.

The Minnesota Historic Farms Study records:

- 10 vegetable canning/preserving companies in 1910;
- 37 vegetable canning factories by 1947;
- major processing operations associated with places including Le Sueur, Owatonna, and Minneapolis.

Source: [Minnesota Department of Transportation, Historic Farms Study](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/culturalresources/docs/crunit/vol3.pdf).

The Green Giant/Minnesota Valley Canning Company history is also documented in Minnesota Historical Society collections.

Source: [Minnesota Historical Society, Green Giant archival collection](https://storage.googleapis.com/mnhs-finding-aids-public/library/findaids/01270.html).

## Why the history matters

The point is not to romanticize the old canning industry.

The point is to document a historical pattern in which agricultural production, processing, labor, transportation, and community life were economically connected.

OurTown asks whether new industries can create a comparable network using today's technologies and markets.
