# Carbon Credits

CarbonShare identifies carbon credits as a possible mechanism connecting corporate climate goals with projects that reduce, avoid, or remove greenhouse-gas emissions.

## The Farmer Question

> **What do carbon credits mean for the farmer?**

That question moves the discussion from corporate goals toward practical agricultural economics.

Potential connections include [[Carbon Sequestration]], [[Biochar]], [[Energy Independence]], and land-management practices.

## Questions of Credibility

A practical carbon-credit system requires clear approaches to measurement, verification, permanence, ownership, and payment.
