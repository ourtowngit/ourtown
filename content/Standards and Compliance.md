# Standards and Compliance

The proposed pilot includes testing protocols aligned with industry SAF fuel standards, GREET, and Minnesota's regulatory framework.

## Testing Objectives

The protocols are intended to address:

- Safety.
- Quality.
- Environmental compliance.
- Fuel standards.
- Sustainability measurement.

## Monitoring

The sustainable development plan calls for monitoring and reporting frameworks consistent with regional and global SAF certification standards.

## Continuous Improvement

Testing and monitoring are intended to provide information that can be used to refine the prototype, feedstock system, processing methods, and future network design.
