# Oilseed Feedstocks

The pilot program is intended to establish processing parameters for oilseed crops and other low-carbon feedstocks aligned with Minnesota's SAF priorities.

## Minnesota Focus

The whitepaper identifies winter camelina as a primary feedstock of interest, particularly as a double crop or relay crop.

Other agricultural biomass and low-carbon feedstocks are also to be explored.

## Feedstock Development

The proposed program includes:

- Modeling feedstock supply-chain feasibility.
- Testing production and processing parameters.
- Developing soil-health and water-quality guidelines.
- Exploring carbon sequestration practices.
- Establishing stable sourcing and pricing relationships.
