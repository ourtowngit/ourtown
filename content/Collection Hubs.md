# Collection Hubs

The whitepaper proposes a network of small localized hubs with rail access.

These hubs would aggregate regional feedstocks before movement to processing facilities, helping stabilize availability and reduce supply-chain risks.

## Functions

Collection hubs can support:

- Feedstock aggregation.
- Storage.
- Rail loading.
- Local farm relationships.
- Supply coordination.
- Connections to cooperative operations.

## Network Logic

The hub model connects [[Oilseed Feedstocks]] to the [[Prototype Processing Facility]] and ultimately to downstream SAF markets.

The proposed geography follows the [[Highway 212 Corridor]] and can extend through the [[Rail Network to the Dakotas]].
