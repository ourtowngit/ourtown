# How Can Farmers Benefit

The central practical question behind OurTown is how family farms can participate in markets created by corporate climate initiatives.

Possible areas include:

- [[Carbon Credits]]
- [[Biochar]]
- [[Carbon Sequestration]]
- [[Energy Independence]]
- [[Value-Added Processing]]
- [[Wasted Industrial Heat]]

The larger opportunity may be combining several activities rather than relying exclusively on carbon-credit revenue.

See also: [[The Opportunity for Family Farms]], [[Rural Value Creation]], [[Farm Cooperative Model]]
