# Farm-Town Infrastructure

The whitepaper emphasizes that many declining rural towns still retain infrastructure that can support redevelopment.

## Existing Assets

The proposed strategy can make use of:

- Rail infrastructure.
- Rural town infrastructure.
- Existing housing.
- Industrial and commercial sites.
- Municipal utilities.
- Agricultural land.
- Transportation corridors.

The whitepaper specifically notes municipal power plants in Glencoe and Granite Falls that provide electric power for commercial and industrial operations at reduced rates.

## Reuse and Regeneration

The objective is to connect existing infrastructure to new agricultural, bio-energy, and business activity rather than treating rural redevelopment as entirely new construction.
