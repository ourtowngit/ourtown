# How Could Wasted Industrial Heat Be Used

The CarbonShare notes identify wasted industrial heat as an opportunity but do not yet define a specific project.

## Questions

- Where is usable industrial heat generated?
- How much energy is available?
- At what temperature?
- How far can it economically be transported?
- Which agricultural processes could use it?
- Could it support greenhouses or crop drying?
- Could it integrate with biomass or renewable energy?
- Who would own and operate recovery infrastructure?
