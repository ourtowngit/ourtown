# Corporate Climate Demand

Large corporate sustainability commitments create a potential source of demand for carbon reduction, removal, and lower-carbon energy.

Microsoft is a useful documented example. Its 2020 commitment and 2025 progress reports show both the scale of corporate ambition and the difficulty of achieving it.

Sources: [Microsoft 2020](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2020/01/16/microsoft-will-be-carbon-negative-by-2030/) and [Microsoft 2025](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/02/13/progress-on-the-road-to-2030/).

OurTown asks how some of that demand could connect with rural projects.
