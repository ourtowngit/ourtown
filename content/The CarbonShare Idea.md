# The CarbonShare Idea

CarbonShare begins with a practical question: **how can family farms and rural communities benefit from corporate efforts to reduce or remove carbon emissions?**

The notes connect several possible pathways:

- [[Carbon Credits]]
- [[Biochar]]
- [[Carbon Sequestration]]
- [[Energy Independence]]
- [[Wasted Industrial Heat]]
- [[Value-Added Processing]]

## A Platform for Action

The underlying idea is to make CarbonShare a platform for organizing information, questions, partnerships, and practical projects.

The stated ambition is to develop a logical, reasonable plan of action that can benefit both farmer and corporate interests.
