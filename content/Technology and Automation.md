# Technology and Automation

The whitepaper calls for integrating 21st-century technologies with traditional and contemporary farming practices.

## Agricultural Technology

The initiative proposes partnerships with technology providers specializing in:

- Precision agriculture.
- Bio-processing.
- Renewable energy integration.
- Robotic and automated farming systems.

## Processing Technology

The prototype is intended to test next-generation bio-crude processing as well as oilseed processing, pelletizing, and localized bio-diesel production.

## Modeling

Computer modeling and economic projections are proposed to evaluate:

- Production capacity.
- Feedstock supply feasibility.
- Facility design.
- Cost competitiveness.
