# What Carbon Credits Mean for Farmers

CarbonShare starts from the farmer rather than the corporate buyer.

The central question is:

> **What does a carbon market actually mean for the farmer?**

## Potential Benefits to Explore

Carbon-related projects could potentially create:

- Additional revenue.
- New markets for agricultural practices.
- Investment in land stewardship.
- Support for energy independence.
- Investment in local processing.

## Questions Before Participation

Farmers need understandable answers about measurement, verification, costs, contracts, payment, permanence, land-management requirements, and ownership of credits.
