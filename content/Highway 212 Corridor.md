# Highway 212 Corridor

The whitepaper identifies the Highway 212 corridor as a geographic spine for a proposed agricultural processing and SAF network.

## What can be verified

The Twin Cities & Western Railroad operates a regional network connecting the Twin Cities with western Minnesota and South Dakota. TC&W describes approximately 229 miles of railroad and connections with major Class I railroads.

Source: [Twin Cities & Western Railroad](https://tcwr.net/new-to-tcw/where-we-move/).

A current transportation document also places TC&W infrastructure alongside the Highway 212 corridor in the Norwood Young America–Cologne area.

## Freight and economic development

A Highway 212 freight study interviewed businesses in the corridor and found that many used rail for at least some inputs or finished products.

Source: [US 212 Freight Generator Interviews](https://projects.srfconsulting.com/build/US212FreightGeneratorInterviews.pdf).

## Important distinction

The corridor should not be described as though Highway 212 and Pine Bend Refinery are directly connected by TC&W track.

The more accurate concept is:

**farm communities → Highway 212 / regional rail → Twin Cities freight network → downstream fuel infrastructure**
