# Highway 212 Corridor

The pilot program leverages the strategic advantages of the Highway 212 corridor together with rail access via the Twin Cities & Western Railroad (TCWR).

## Strategic Role

The corridor offers:

- Access to existing rural communities and infrastructure.
- Rail connectivity.
- Proximity to the Twin Cities metropolitan area.
- A potential pathway to the Pine Bend Refinery.
- A foundation for westward regional expansion.

## Pilot Geography

The whitepaper identifies the 212 corridor as the setting for proposed pilot projects and calls for consensus and approval for pilot projects along the corridor.
