# Pilot Project Roadmap

The whitepaper's action plan can be organized into a sequence of linked workstreams.

## 1. Define the Pilot

- Establish processing-facility parameters.
- Focus initially on relevant Minnesota feedstocks such as winter camelina.
- Explore other low-carbon feedstocks.
- Identify a demonstration site in McLeod County.

## 2. Model the System

- Model facility capacity.
- Evaluate feedstock supply feasibility.
- Project economics and cost competitiveness.
- Develop sustainability metrics.

## 3. Build Partnerships

Engage development organizations, cooperatives, universities, technology providers, refineries, SAF purchasers, and potential investors.

## 4. Establish Testing

Develop protocols for SAF-related standards, GREET, Minnesota regulations, safety, quality, and environmental compliance.

## 5. Develop Conservation Guidelines

Incorporate soil health, water quality, and carbon sequestration practices.

## 6. Prepare for Scale

Use the pilot to inform [[Collection Hubs]], [[Rail Network to the Dakotas]], and a broader [[SAF Supply Chain]].
