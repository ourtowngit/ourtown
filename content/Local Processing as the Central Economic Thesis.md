# Local Processing as the Central Economic Thesis

This is one of the central propositions of CarbonShare:

> **More agricultural value can remain in rural communities when more of the processing occurs near the farm.**

## From commodity to system

A farm can sell a raw commodity into a distant market.

A regional system can potentially add additional layers of value:

**farm → aggregation → processing → oil → fuel → meal → carbon products → energy → logistics**

Each layer creates a potential market, service, job, or ownership opportunity.

## What must be proven

The thesis is not automatically true for every commodity or facility.

A project must demonstrate:

- competitive farm-gate pricing;
- viable processing economics;
- reliable feedstock supply;
- markets for co-products;
- efficient logistics;
- capital availability;
- appropriate scale;
- local ownership or participation;
- compliance with fuel and carbon standards.

USDA research on biobased products supports the broader proposition that biorefineries and biobased industries can create agricultural markets and rural economic activity.
