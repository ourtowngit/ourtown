# Food and Agricultural Diversification

The whitepaper places bio-energy development within a broader strategy for agricultural diversification.

## Historical Precedent

Minnesota farm communities once supported substantial food-processing industries. The whitepaper references more than 150 canneries operating in the 1960s, with Green Giant identified as the largest.

## Contemporary Opportunities

The proposed rural development model includes:

- Local fruits and vegetables.
- Greenhouses.
- Orchards.
- Small farms.
- Food processing.
- Packaging.
- Freezing.
- Canning.
- Agricultural technology services.

## Relationship to Bio-Energy

The same local infrastructure that supports oilseed collection and processing can contribute to a broader agricultural economy.
