# Pine Bend Refinery Connection

The whitepaper identifies proximity to the Pine Bend Refinery in Rosemount, Minnesota, as a strategic advantage for the proposed network.

## Role in the Supply Chain

The project anticipates coordination with regional refining and fuel infrastructure to support downstream processing and SAF supply.

The action plan also calls for early planning around connections with existing fuel distribution pipelines and blending facilities.

## Regional Position

The combination of the [[Highway 212 Corridor]], Twin Cities-area proximity, and downstream infrastructure is intended to create a pathway from agricultural feedstocks to larger fuel markets.
