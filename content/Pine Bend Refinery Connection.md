# Pine Bend Refinery Connection

Pine Bend is an existing major fuel-infrastructure node in Minnesota.

MnDOT reports refinery capacity of approximately **335,000 barrels per day** and notes that refined products include aircraft fuel distributed through pipeline infrastructure.

Source: [MnDOT freight system documentation](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/minnesotago/freight/3-assets-conditions-performance.html).

The refinery's own materials identify jet fuel among its products and report more than 1,000 employees.

Source: [Pine Bend Refinery](https://pinebendrefinery.com/).

## What OurTown proposes

The whitepaper identifies proximity to existing downstream infrastructure as a strategic consideration.

The correct claim is not that TC&W directly serves Pine Bend.

Instead, Pine Bend represents an existing regional **fuel and aviation-energy infrastructure node** within the broader Twin Cities logistics system.
