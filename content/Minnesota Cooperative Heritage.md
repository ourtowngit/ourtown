# Minnesota Cooperative Heritage

Minnesota has a long agricultural cooperative tradition.

The Minnesota Historical Society documents Oliver Kelley and the formation of the National Grange in 1867, while University of Minnesota agricultural-economics records document the growth of farmer cooperatives in the state.

Sources:

- [Minnesota Historical Society — Oliver Kelley](https://www.mnhs.org/kelleyfarm/learn/oliver-kelley)
- [Minnesota Historical Society — The Grange](https://www.mnhs.org/newspapers/hub/the-grange-advance)
- [University of Minnesota — Minnesota cooperative history](https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/163292/files/mn-ag-econ-398.pdf)

## Scale

A University of Minnesota historical review reported more than a thousand farmer marketing, supply, and related-service cooperatives in Minnesota in the mid-20th century.

USDA's 2022 data counted 143 agricultural cooperatives headquartered in Minnesota.

See [[Minnesota Agricultural Cooperatives Today]].
