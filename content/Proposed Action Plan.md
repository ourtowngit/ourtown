# Proposed Action Plan

The proposed action plan integrates 21st-century technology with agricultural production, processing, infrastructure, education, and rural development.

## 1. Pilot Projects

Focus on Minnesota-relevant feedstocks, establish processing parameters, model economics and supply, develop testing protocols, and identify a McLeod County demonstration site.

See [[Pilot Project Roadmap]] and [[Farm Conservation Sub-Division]].

## 2. Collaborative Partnerships

Develop relationships with development organizations, agricultural cooperatives, universities, sustainability nonprofits, technology partners, downstream SAF refineries, and SAF purchasers.

See [[Partnerships and Stakeholders]].

## 3. Job Creation and Education

Create pathways across agronomy, processing, quality assurance, environmental monitoring, logistics, and related fields. Develop technical curricula and community outreach.

See [[Workforce and Education]].

## 4. Sustainable Development

Implement [[Rail-Oriented Development]] and Farm Development Guidelines, develop model projects, promote circular agricultural businesses, and establish sustainability monitoring.

See [[Sustainable Development]].

## 5. Bio-energy-Based Cooperative

Form a cooperative to aggregate supplies, advance processing, represent farm interests, and support energy independence.

See [[Farm Cooperative Model]].

## 6. Additional Strategic Focus Areas

Plan early for infrastructure compatibility, financial viability, risk mitigation, and ongoing research.

See [[Strategic Focus Areas]].
