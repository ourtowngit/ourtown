# Proposed Action Plan

The future of farming requires a strategic approach integrating 21st-century technologies. The proposed action plan is broken down into several key areas:

1. **Pilot Projects**: Focus on feedstocks relevant to Minnesota's climate (e.g., winter camelina as a double or relay crop). Identify a demonstration site in McLeod County for a "Farm Conservation Sub-Division".
2. **Collaborative Partnerships**: Foster partnerships with organizations, universities (like the University of Minnesota's Forever Green Initiative), and downstream SAF refineries.
3. **Job Creation and Education**: Partner with technical schools for curricula focused on land stewardship, bio-energy technologies, and emerging robotic farming systems.
4. **Sustainable Development**: Implement [[Rail-Oriented Development]] guidelines and Farm Development Guidelines incentivizing high-productivity small farm opportunities (5-40 acres).
5. **Bio-energy-Based Cooperative**: Form a cooperative to aggregate supplies and represent farm interests. See [[Farm Cooperative Model]].
6. **Additional Strategic Focus Areas**: Integrate early planning for infrastructure compatibility, including connections with existing fuel distribution pipelines (e.g., Flint Hills Pine Bend refinery).
