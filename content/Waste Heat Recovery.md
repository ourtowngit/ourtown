# Waste Heat Recovery

Industrial processes frequently reject energy as heat.

The U.S. Department of Energy says **20–50% of industrial energy input can be lost as waste heat**, depending on the industry and process.

Source: [U.S. Department of Energy, Waste Heat Recovery Basics](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/ito/waste-heat-recovery-basics).

## Potential uses

Recovered heat can potentially support:

- process heating;
- drying;
- hot water;
- district or nearby building heat;
- steam generation;
- power generation;
- agricultural processing.

## The OurTown question

A rural processing system could be designed so that one facility's waste stream becomes another facility's energy input.

That creates a possible local loop:

**processing → waste heat → drying / heating → lower energy demand → lower operating cost**

The economics are site-specific. Temperature, distance, timing, equipment cost, available heat, and energy prices all matter.
