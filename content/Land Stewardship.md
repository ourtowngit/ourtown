# Land Stewardship

The pilot program includes farm conservation guidelines incorporating soil health management, water quality protection, and carbon sequestration practices.

## Why It Matters

Land stewardship is integrated into the proposed feedstock system rather than treated as a separate activity.

The plan connects stewardship practices with:

- Sustainable feedstock production.
- Environmental compliance.
- Carbon credit programs and incentives.
- Long-term agricultural productivity.

## Demonstration

The proposed [[Farm Conservation Sub-Division]] in McLeod County would provide a setting for modeling sustainability metrics and community impacts.
