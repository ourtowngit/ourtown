# Corporate Climate Commitments

The CarbonShare notes begin with the observation that major corporations are establishing clear goals around reducing carbon dioxide emissions.

This raises a practical question for agriculture:

> What markets or partnerships could emerge when corporations seek measurable carbon reductions or removals?

Potential connections include [[Carbon Credits]], [[Carbon Sequestration]], [[Biochar]], [[Wasted Industrial Heat]], and [[Energy Independence]].

The CarbonShare challenge is connecting corporate demand with projects that farmers and rural communities can actually participate in.
