# Carbon Sequestration

CarbonShare identifies sequestering carbon as one possible pathway for connecting agricultural activity with corporate carbon goals.

## Agricultural Connection

Farms manage land and biomass, creating opportunities to explore practices that may increase carbon storage in soils, vegetation, or durable products.

Potential connections include [[Biochar]], soil-management practices, biomass utilization, and [[Carbon Credits]].

## Measurement Matters

Any carbon-sequestration market requires credible methods for determining how much carbon is stored and for how long.
