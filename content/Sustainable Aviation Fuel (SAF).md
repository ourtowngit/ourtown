# Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)

The whitepaper identifies growing SAF demand as an opportunity to create agricultural markets and stimulate rural economic development.

IATA says aviation has committed to net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 and estimates SAF could contribute up to 65% of the emissions reductions needed on that pathway.

In 2026, IATA estimates global SAF production at about 2.4 million tonnes, or 0.8% of aviation fuel use.

Sources: [IATA SAF](https://www.iata.org/en/programs/sustainability/sustainable-aviation-fuel-saf/) and [IATA 2026 production estimate](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/06-06-saf-production-volumes-still-disappointing/).

## Why agriculture matters

The OurTown/SAF proposal investigates:

- feedstock cultivation;
- farmer-led aggregation;
- local processing;
- co-products such as meal;
- cooperative ownership;
- rail logistics;
- connections to downstream fuel infrastructure.

The current evidence supports researching these possibilities. It does not establish that any specific farm, feedstock, or facility will qualify or be profitable.
