# Bio-Crude and Seed Oil

The prototype is intended to supply raw seed oil for downstream refining into [[Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)]] while also testing next-generation bio-crude processing technology.

## Processing Outputs

The proposed network may produce:

- Raw seed oil.
- SAF components.
- Bio-crude.
- Protein-rich meal.
- Residual hull and biomass products.

## Why Local Processing

Localized processing can move value-added activity closer to farms and communities. The whitepaper connects this approach with higher farm value capture, new sustainable markets, and rural economic development.
