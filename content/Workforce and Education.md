# Workforce and Education

The whitepaper identifies workforce development as a core part of rural revitalization.

## Employment Pathways

Potential roles include:

- Agronomy.
- Bio-fuel processing.
- Quality assurance.
- Environmental monitoring.
- Logistics.
- Construction.
- Technology.
- Research and development.
- Management.

## Education Partnerships

The proposal calls for partnerships with technical schools, colleges, and universities to develop curricula in:

- Land stewardship.
- Bio-energy technologies.
- Sustainable agriculture.
- Robotic and automated farming systems.

## Community Outreach

Community outreach can raise awareness of the SAF opportunity and support informed local participation in the development process.
