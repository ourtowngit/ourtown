# Aviation Industry and Emissions Goals

Aviation has a clearly stated industry-level goal, but it is important to identify who made the commitment.

## IATA

The International Air Transport Association says the aviation sector has committed to achieving **net-zero carbon emissions by 2050**.

IATA identifies SAF as the most important decarbonization lever in the coming decades and says SAF could contribute up to **65% of the emissions reductions needed** to reach net zero.

Source: [IATA, Sustainable Aviation Fuel](https://www.iata.org/en/programs/sustainability/sustainable-aviation-fuel-saf/).

## The supply problem

IATA estimates that 2026 global SAF production will be about **2.4 million tonnes**, representing **0.8% of aviation fuel use**. IATA says roughly **500 million tonnes per year** would be needed by 2050 on its pathway.

Source: [IATA, “SAF Production Volumes Still Disappointing,” June 6, 2026](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/06-06-saf-production-volumes-still-disappointing/).

## Individual airline examples

The aviation transition is also expressed through airline-specific goals.

- **United Airlines** has a net-zero-by-2050 goal and reports a 50% reduction target for GHG intensity by 2035 from a 2019 baseline. [United Corporate Impact](https://corporateimpact.united.com/environmental-sustainability/our-environmental-strategy/)
- **American Airlines** has stated a goal of using SAF for 10% of its jet fuel by 2030 and has reported SAF purchase commitments. [American Airlines Sustainability](https://www.aa.com/i18n/customer-service/about-us/corporate-responsibility/sustainability.jsp)
- **Delta Air Lines** reports SAF-use targets of 10% in 2030, 35% in 2035, and 95% in 2050. [Delta ESG Hub](https://esghub.delta.com/)
- **Alaska Airlines** has a 2040 net-zero-carbon goal and identifies SAF as part of its strategy. [Alaska Airlines sustainability](https://news.alaskaair.com/sustainability/).

These commitments do not guarantee a market for any particular feedstock or facility. They establish a documented long-term demand signal that CarbonShare can study.
