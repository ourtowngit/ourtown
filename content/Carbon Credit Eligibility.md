# Carbon Credit Eligibility

A carbon-removal activity and a carbon credit are not the same thing.

A project may physically remove or store carbon without automatically producing a marketable credit.

## Questions a project must answer

- What is the baseline?
- Is the activity additional?
- How is carbon quantified?
- How durable is the storage?
- What emissions occur during production and transport?
- What monitoring, reporting, and verification system applies?
- Who owns the environmental attribute?
- Which registry or methodology is being used?

## Biochar example

Verra's VM0044 methodology provides one established framework for biochar projects.

Source: [Verra, VM0044](https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0044-biochar-utilization-in-soil-and-non-soil-applications-v1-2/).

CarbonShare should therefore treat carbon credits as a **potential revenue stream subject to project qualification**, rather than as guaranteed farm income.
