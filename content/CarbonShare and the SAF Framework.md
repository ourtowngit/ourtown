# CarbonShare and the SAF Framework

The CarbonShare ideas connect naturally with the broader rural bio-energy and Sustainable Aviation Fuel framework.

## Shared Themes

Both concepts explore:

- [[Energy Independence]]
- Agricultural feedstocks.
- Local processing.
- Carbon management.
- Rural economic development.
- Cooperative structures.
- Existing infrastructure.
- New markets for farmers.

## SAF Connection

The SAF framework proposes oilseed collection hubs and processing facilities connected by rail.

## Carbon Connection

CarbonShare adds questions around carbon credits, biochar, sequestration, and corporate demand.
