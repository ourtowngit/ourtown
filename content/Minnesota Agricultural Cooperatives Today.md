# Minnesota Agricultural Cooperatives Today

The earlier CarbonShare draft referred to more than 60 active agricultural cooperatives in Minnesota.

Current USDA data gives us a better documented figure.

USDA's 2022 Agricultural Cooperative Statistics counted **143 agricultural cooperatives headquartered in Minnesota**:

- 63 marketing cooperatives;
- 80 supply/service cooperatives.

Source: [USDA Rural Development, Agricultural Cooperative Statistics 2022](https://www.rd.usda.gov/sites/default/files/SR86-AgriculturalCooperativeStatistics-2022-UnpublishedReport.pdf).

## Why the cooperative count matters

CarbonShare is not proposing cooperation as an entirely new institutional idea.

Minnesota already has a long history of farmers using cooperative structures for:

- purchasing;
- marketing;
- storage;
- processing;
- finance;
- shared services.

The proposed model adapts that institutional history to new markets in fuel, carbon, and energy.
