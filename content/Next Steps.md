# Next Steps

The whitepaper identifies several immediate next steps for moving from concept toward coordinated action.

## Resources and Funding

Identify funding and other resources, including:

- State programs.
- Federal programs.
- Commercial banking.
- Private investment.
- Agricultural stakeholders.
- Community stakeholders.

See [[Financing and Incentives]].

## Steering Group

Form a steering group to guide and coordinate efforts.

See [[Leadership and Coordination]].

## Cooperative and Community Development

Build consensus around:

- A Bio-Fuel Cooperative.
- A Rail Oriented Community Development initiative.

See [[Farm Cooperative Model]] and [[Rail-Oriented Development]].

## Pilot Projects

Seek consensus and approval for pilot projects in the 212 corridor.

See [[Highway 212 Corridor]] and [[Pilot Project Roadmap]].

## Rural Communication

Produce an information video for rural communities, potential participants, and stakeholders describing current conditions, the opportunity, and the proposed approach to restoring farm/town synergy through collaborative planning.

See also: [[Proposed Action Plan]], [[Rural Revitalization]]
