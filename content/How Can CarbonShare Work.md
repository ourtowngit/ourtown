# How Can CarbonShare Work?

The notes describe CarbonShare as a logical platform for addressing climate, carbon, energy, and rural economic questions.

## Knowledge

Build interconnected resources explaining [[Carbon Credits]], [[Carbon Sequestration]], [[Biochar]], [[Energy Independence]], and corporate climate commitments.

## Projects

Identify practical projects where carbon, agriculture, and energy intersect.

## Partnerships

Connect farmers and rural communities with corporate purchasers, technology providers, financial institutions, researchers, local governments, and cooperatives.

## The Goal

Develop a logical, reasonable plan of action that can benefit both farmer and corporate interests.
