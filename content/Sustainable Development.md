# Sustainable Development

The proposed development framework combines low-carbon logistics, agricultural diversification, local processing, and sustainability monitoring.

## Rail and Logistics

The project proposes documenting and implementing [[Rail-Oriented Development]] guidelines to support efficient, low-carbon transport of SAF feedstocks and products.

## Farm Development

Farm Development Guidelines would encourage productive 5–40 acre agricultural opportunities, biodiversity, local production, greenhouses, orchards, and advanced automated systems.

## Circular Agriculture

The plan also proposes cooperative facilities for:

- Storage.
- Processing.
- Packaging.
- Freezing.
- Canning.

These activities can add value to agricultural output and support community resilience.

## Monitoring

The whitepaper calls for sustainability monitoring and reporting consistent with regional and global SAF certification standards.
