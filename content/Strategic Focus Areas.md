# Strategic Focus Areas

The whitepaper identifies several strategic areas that should be addressed early in project development.

## Infrastructure Compatibility

Plan for connections with existing fuel distribution pipelines and blending facilities, including coordination with regional hubs such as the Pine Bend Refinery area.

## Financial Viability and Risk

Conduct financial viability and risk assessment studies, identify economic hurdles, and develop mitigation strategies.

## Research and Technology

Maintain research collaborations with academic institutions and industry leaders to track:

- SAF production technologies.
- Policy environments.
- Market trends.
- Processing developments.

## Market Connections

Develop relationships with downstream refineries, suppliers, airline industry groups, and SAF purchasers.
