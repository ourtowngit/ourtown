# The Project and Objectives

The primary objective is to establish a prototype processing facility to serve as a scalable model for a comprehensive network of oil-seed collection hubs and processing stations. This facility will supply raw seed oil for downstream refining into [[Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)]].

## Strategic Location
This pilot program leverages proximity to the Twin Cities metropolitan area and the Pine Bend Refinery in Rosemount MN. Extending this rail network westward into the Dakotas via TCWR, Sisseton Milbank Railroad (SMR), and Red River Valley & Western Railway (RRVW) connections strengthens the regional scale.

## Secondary Objectives
- **Animal Feed:** Testing the pelletizing of residual protein-rich meal byproduct for cattle and poultry feed.
- **Farm Energy:** Testing localized processes for conversion of raw seed oils to bio-diesel for direct farm use, crop drying, and fuel for micro-steam turbine generators.
