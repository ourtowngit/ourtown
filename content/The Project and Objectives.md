# The Project and Objectives

The primary objective is to establish a prototype processing facility that can serve as a scalable model for a broader network of oilseed collection hubs and processing stations.

The prototype is intended to supply raw seed oil for downstream refining into [[Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)]] while testing and advancing next-generation bio-crude processing technology.

## Secondary Objectives

- Test pelletizing of residual protein-rich meal for cattle and poultry feed.
- Test localized conversion of raw seed oils to bio-diesel for direct farm use.
- Explore applications of residual biomass for crop drying and micro-steam turbine generators.
- Demonstrate how localized processing can connect farm production with regional energy and fuel markets.

## Strategic Location

The pilot leverages proximity to the Twin Cities metropolitan area and the [[Pine Bend Refinery Connection|Pine Bend Refinery]] in Rosemount, Minnesota.

Its rail strategy connects the [[Highway 212 Corridor]] with the [[Rail Network to the Dakotas]], using the Twin Cities & Western Railroad (TCWR), Sisseton Milbank Railroad (SMR), and Red River Valley & Western Railway (RRVW).
