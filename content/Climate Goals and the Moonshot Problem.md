# Climate Goals and the Moonshot Problem

Microsoft's 2025 sustainability update provides the phrase that anchors this part of OurTown.

In 2020, Microsoft described its sustainability goals as a **moonshot**. In 2025, Microsoft said that “the moon has gotten further away,” while also saying that it remained committed to its goals.

Source: [Microsoft, “Progress on the road to 2030,” February 13, 2025](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/02/13/progress-on-the-road-to-2030/).

## The OurTown interpretation

The useful lesson is not that ambitious goals are impossible.

It is that a large goal requires many practical systems beneath it:

**goal → demand → project → measurement → financing → production → logistics → market**

OurTown asks whether rural agricultural systems can become part of those practical systems.
