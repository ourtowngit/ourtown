# Biochar

Biochar is one possible bridge between agricultural biomass, carbon removal, soil management, and local processing.

## Carbon-removal methodology

Verra's **VM0044 — Biochar Utilization in Soil and Non-Soil Applications** is an active methodology for quantifying carbon dioxide removals associated with converting waste biomass into biochar.

Source: [Verra, VM0044 v1.2](https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0044-biochar-utilization-in-soil-and-non-soil-applications-v1-2/).

The methodology addresses issues including feedstock sourcing, production, energy use, transport, application, and carbon stability.

## What this means for farmers

The existence of a methodology does not mean that every biochar project automatically creates a credit.

A project still has to satisfy the relevant methodology, monitoring, documentation, additionality, permanence, and verification requirements.
