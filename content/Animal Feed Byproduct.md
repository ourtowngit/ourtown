# Animal Feed Byproduct

The prototype facility is intended to test pelletizing residual protein-rich meal for cattle and poultry feed.

## Research and Testing

The whitepaper calls for testing:

- Pelletizing methods.
- Handling practices.
- Distribution practices.
- Local and regional uses of residual material.

The objective is to capture additional value from the processing stream rather than treating residual material solely as waste.

## Broader Circular Economy

Byproduct utilization is part of a larger strategy involving cooperative storage, processing, packaging, freezing, canning, and other value-added agricultural activities.
