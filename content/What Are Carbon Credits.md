# What Are Carbon Credits

One of the fundamental OurTown questions is simply: **what are carbon credits?**

## Questions

- How is a reduction or removal measured?
- Who verifies it?
- How long does the carbon remain stored?
- Who owns the environmental attribute?
- How does value reach the farmer?
- What does participation cost?
- What obligations does the farmer assume?

These questions should be answered clearly before presenting any particular carbon-credit opportunity as established or guaranteed.
