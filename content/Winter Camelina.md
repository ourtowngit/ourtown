# Winter Camelina

Winter camelina is identified in the whitepaper as a primary feedstock for initial pilot work because it can be considered as a double crop or relay crop in Minnesota's agricultural system.

## Role in the Pilot

The proposed pilot would:

- Establish and document oilseed processing parameters.
- Evaluate feedstock supply feasibility.
- Model production capacity and economics.
- Test processing pathways relevant to SAF.
- Explore agricultural practices that support soil health and water quality.

Winter camelina is therefore part of the broader effort to diversify agricultural markets while connecting existing farm operations to renewable fuel production.
