# Energy Independence

OurTown asks whether farms and rural communities can produce more of the energy they consume rather than depending entirely on externally purchased fuels.

## Grow Your Own Fuel

The notes frame this simply as:

> **Grow your own fuel.**

Historically, farms produced energy through feed for draft animals, hay, firewood, and crop residues. Modern equivalents could include agricultural biomass, biofuels, renewable electricity, and local processing.

## Economic Connection

Energy independence can affect farm input costs, rural infrastructure, local businesses, energy security, and value retained in communities.
