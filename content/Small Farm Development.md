# Small Farm Development

The whitepaper proposes creating opportunities for small farms alongside established larger-scale agricultural operations.

## 5–40 Acre Opportunities

The proposed Farm Development Guidelines would encourage higher-productivity agricultural opportunities on 5–40 acre sites.

Potential examples include:

- Local produce.
- Biodiversity-focused operations.
- Greenhouses.
- Orchards.
- Farming operations using advanced automated planting and harvesting technology.

## Connection to the Larger System

Small farms are presented as part of a diversified rural economy rather than a replacement for established farms.

Farmer-led processing, packaging, and distribution systems can create new markets across farm sizes.
