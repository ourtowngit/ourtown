# Farm Bio-Diesel and Local Energy

Farm-produced biodiesel belongs to the broader question of [[Energy Independence]]: how much of the energy required by agriculture can be produced from agricultural resources themselves?

This connects [[Grow Your Own Fuel]], [[Oilseed Feedstocks]], [[Value-Added Processing]], and [[Farm Cooperative Model]].

## Questions to resolve
- Which crops and residues are technically and economically suitable?
- What processing scale makes sense for individual farms versus cooperatives?
- How should fuel quality and regulatory requirements be handled?
- How does local fuel production interact with [[Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)]]?
- What carbon accounting is required if fuel production also generates carbon-market value?

See [[Closing the Energy Loop]].
