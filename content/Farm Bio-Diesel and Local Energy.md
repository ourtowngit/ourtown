# Farm Bio-Diesel and Local Energy

A secondary objective of the project is to test localized processes for converting raw seed oils to bio-diesel for direct farm use and local energy production.

## Potential Applications

The whitepaper identifies:

- Direct farm fuel.
- Crop drying.
- Farm heating using pelletized biomass.
- Micro-steam turbine generators.

## Closing the Energy Loop

Localized bio-fuel production is framed as a way to increase energy independence for farms and rural communities.

The approach connects agricultural production, processing, and local energy consumption instead of treating fuel solely as an external input.
