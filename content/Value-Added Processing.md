# Value-Added Processing

A recurring CarbonShare idea is to get production and value-added processes back into small towns.

## From Commodity to Product

Instead of shipping raw agricultural commodities away for all subsequent processing, some processing could occur closer to where materials are produced.

Potential areas include biofuels, biochar, biomass, food processing, agricultural products, and energy services.

## Why It Matters

Local processing can create opportunities for employment, entrepreneurship, farm income, skilled trades, infrastructure investment, and local economic activity.
