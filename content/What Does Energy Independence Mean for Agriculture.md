# What Does Energy Independence Mean for Agriculture?

For a farm, energy independence might mean producing some of its own fuel or electricity.

For a rural community, it might mean retaining more energy-related spending locally.

For a region, it could involve a network of agricultural energy production and processing.

## Questions

- Can farms produce some of their own fuel?
- Can agricultural residues become energy resources?
- Can oilseed crops support local fuel production?
- Can recovered industrial heat serve agricultural processes?
- Can energy infrastructure become rural economic infrastructure?
