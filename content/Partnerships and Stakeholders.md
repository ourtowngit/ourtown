# Partnerships and Stakeholders

The whitepaper emphasizes transparent engagement with stakeholders and decision-makers throughout planning.

## Potential Partners

The proposed network includes:

- Local and regional development organizations.
- Agricultural cooperatives.
- Universities.
- Sustainability nonprofits.
- Technology providers.
- Downstream SAF refineries and suppliers.
- Airline industry consortia and SAF purchasers.
- Local, county, and state governments.
- Farmers and farm organizations.
- Private and publicly held companies.
- Technical schools and colleges.

## Research Partnerships

The action plan specifically references resources such as the University of Minnesota's Forever Green Initiative and Montana State University's ECON Project.

## Demand-Side Relationships

The proposal calls for connections with the Greater MSP Partnership/SAF Hub, downstream SAF refineries and suppliers, airline industry consortiums, and SAF purchasers to explore demand commitments, off-take agreements, and potential investment.
