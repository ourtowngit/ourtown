# The Prototype Processing Facility

The prototype processing facility is the central demonstration project in the whitepaper.

Its purpose is to establish a practical model for a larger network of [[Collection Hubs]] and processing stations.

## Primary Functions

The facility is intended to:

- Receive and process oilseed feedstocks.
- Produce raw seed oil for downstream SAF refining.
- Test next-generation bio-crude processing.
- Demonstrate localized processing at useful scale.
- Test handling and distribution of residual materials.

## Secondary Functions

The facility can also serve as a testing hub for:

- Pelletized protein-rich meal for cattle and poultry.
- Pelletized hull material for farm heating.
- Crop-drying fuel.
- Fuel for micro-steam turbine generators.
- Localized bio-diesel production.

## Scaling

The prototype is intended to inform a network rather than operate as an isolated facility. Its results would inform [[Collection Hubs]], [[SAF Supply Chain]], and the [[Pilot Project Roadmap]].
