# Rail-Oriented Development

The whitepaper uses Minnesota's historical rail development pattern as a framework for rural regeneration.

Railroads opened agricultural territory, anchored settlement patterns, and helped establish towns around stations and depots. Rail access supported both the movement of farm inputs and the movement of agricultural products to markets.

## A Contemporary Application

The proposal is to reuse existing rail infrastructure as part of a low-carbon agricultural and bio-energy network.

- [[Highway 212 Corridor]]

## Rural Development Connection

The rail strategy is not only a transportation strategy. It is intended to support farm communities as centers of commerce, processing, employment, and rural regeneration.
