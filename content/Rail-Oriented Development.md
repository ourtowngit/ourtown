# Rail-Oriented Development

The westward expansion of the 19th century is a defining chapter in Minnesota history. Railroads opened vast lands, anchoring settlement patterns and establishing a vast farm-based agricultural network. Train stations and depots became focal points around which businesses clustered; railroads functioned as the arteries of commerce.

By recognizing this historic rail-oriented development pattern, we can reverse the late 20th-century decline in rural services and re-establish farm communities as centers for rural regeneration and commerce.

This initiative leverages the strategic advantages of the Highway 212 corridor and rail access via the Twin Cities & Western Railroad (TCWR), offering a central and logistically advantageous location for SAF supply.
