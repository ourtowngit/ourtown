# Grow Your Own Fuel

“Grow your own fuel” captures a central CarbonShare idea: agriculture can potentially become part of its own energy supply.

## Historical Roots

Before modern fossil-fuel dependence, farms produced energy indirectly through crops, livestock feed, firewood, and crop residues.

Today, agricultural energy systems can be reconsidered through oilseed crops, bio-diesel, biomass, biochar, renewable electricity, and local processing.

## The Bigger Idea

The objective is not necessarily complete energy self-sufficiency. It is to explore which portions of the farm energy system can be produced locally and economically.
