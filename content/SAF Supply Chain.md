# SAF Supply Chain

The proposed system links agricultural production to downstream SAF refining through localized collection and processing infrastructure.

## Core Flow

**Farms → Collection Hubs → Processing Facilities → Rail Logistics → Downstream Refining → SAF Market**

The model begins with regional aggregation of oilseed feedstocks. Collection hubs stabilize supply before material moves to processing facilities.

Processing can produce:

- Raw seed oil.
- Bio-crude and SAF-related components.
- Protein-rich meal for animal feed.
- Biomass products for energy applications.

## Supply Stability

The [[Farm Cooperative Model]] is intended to aggregate regional feedstock supplies, stabilize market availability, represent farm interests, and support long-term relationships within the commodity supply chain.

## Regional Scale

The [[Rail Network to the Dakotas]] provides a proposed pathway for extending the network westward.
