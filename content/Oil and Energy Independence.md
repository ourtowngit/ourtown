# Oil and Energy Independence

CarbonShare does not need to predict when petroleum will run out to make the energy-independence argument.

A more precise proposition is:

> **Modern agriculture depends heavily on purchased liquid fuels and petroleum-derived inputs, exposing farms and rural communities to fuel prices, supply disruptions, and energy systems controlled beyond the farm gate.**

The question is whether some portion of that exposure can be reduced through locally produced biological energy, renewable electricity, recovered industrial heat, and distributed processing.
