# Economic Development

The whitepaper frames localized bio-energy infrastructure as a rural economic development strategy.

## Value-Added Agriculture

Moving processing, packaging, storage, and other value-added activities closer to agricultural production can retain more economic activity in farm communities.

## Employment

Potential employment spans:

- Entry-level operations.
- Skilled trades.
- Management.
- Technology.
- Research and development.
- Construction.
- Logistics.
- Environmental monitoring.
- Agricultural services.

## Diversification

The proposal links bio-energy development with diversified cropping, local food production, small farms, agricultural technology, and other new businesses.

## Community Effects

The intended result is a more diversified rural economy in which agriculture, processing, transportation, energy, and services reinforce one another.
