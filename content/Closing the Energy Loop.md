# Closing the Energy Loop

“Closing the energy loop” describes the larger system connecting agricultural production, local energy, processing, transportation, carbon value, and end users.

1. **Farm production** → feedstocks and residues.
2. **Collection and logistics** → [[Collection Hubs]] and [[Rail-Oriented Development]].
3. **Local processing** → [[Value-Added Processing]] and [[Prototype Processing Facility]].
4. **Energy products** → [[Farm Bio-Diesel and Local Energy]] and [[Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)]].
5. **Carbon value** → [[Carbon Credits]] and [[Carbon Sequestration]].
6. **Local investment** → [[Rural Value Creation]] and [[Rural Revitalization]].

Each link requires technical, market, regulatory, and financial validation.
